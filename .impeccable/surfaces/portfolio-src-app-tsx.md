# Surface brief: portfolio site

Scope: whole single-page portfolio, Experience mode. Audience: recruiters and developers equally. Constraints: no invented proof, static, keep MotorcycleCursor, Konami mode, ScrollToTop; bikes banner leads; Bricolage Grotesque stays; avoid card grids, corporate polish, cheap orange, low readability.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Lowicz-style layered papercut in shades of orange. Depth comes only from overlapping flat papers, never gradients, blur or shadows. Refuses the centred-hero-plus-equal-cards portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy flat papers: persimmon, tangerine, amber, peach and burnt sienna on a deep ink-brown. Ink-brown type only. Pinked scalloped cut edges, slight rotations, a twelve-fold symmetrical roundel. Tags and labels are cut paper strips.

STORY: A visitor sees the bikes, knows this is Ayush, a software engineering intern at Rivian, then reads work as layers pasted down in order, and reaches out.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Persimmon field. Name set huge left in ink over a symmetric orange roundel; the two-bike banner pasted full width below on a scalloped sheet, slightly rotated; Get in Touch is a cut-paper tab.

FORM: Papercut Roundel (catalog challenger, competitive), chosen by the user from the roll; seed key 6a3599b6.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
