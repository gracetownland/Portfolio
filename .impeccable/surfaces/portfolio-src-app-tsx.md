# Surface brief: portfolio site

Scope: whole single-page portfolio, Experience mode. Audience: recruiters and developers equally. Constraints: no invented proof, static, keep MotorcycleCursor and ScrollToTop; Bricolage Grotesque stays; Konami mode and vines were removed by the user. The user rejected the earlier all-orange papercut direction as ugly and pinned: white ground, orange used only as tasteful, minimal highlights, bikes in the hero card, photo wide and uncropped. A user-pinned direction beats the roll, so no new roll was run.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm white page where orange is only ever a highlight: a rule, a dot, a marker stripe behind a number, one primary button. Refuses the all-orange fields and the centred-hero-plus-equal-cards portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: White and near-white grounds, near-black ink (#171717), orange #f97316 as the only colour, deep orange #c2410c for small orange text, a pale orange #ffedd5 marker for key numbers. Hairline rules, big tight Bricolage headings, rounded hero card, pill buttons, uncoloured gray bullets, thumbnails desaturated until hover.

STORY: A visitor sees the name and role, then the bikes, scans the highlighted numbers, and reaches out through the Contact links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White page. Name huge in ink at top-left of the text column, small orange dot beside the role, a short pitch, an orange Get in Touch pill with two outline pills. Directly below, the two-bike photo at its natural wide proportion in a rounded card, wider than the text column, uncropped.

FORM: User-pinned direction (white with orange highlights), supersedes the dealt Papercut Roundel; seed key 6a3599b6.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
